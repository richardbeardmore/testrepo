Migrations & upgrades

Obsolete systems, migrated with minimal downtime

Ageing control systems carry real risk: unavailable spares, lost production and rising support costs. Our engineers have delivered smooth, efficient migrations time and again — here's how we approach them.

Assess

A detailed assessment of your existing control system: spares availability, impact of lost production, support costs, connectivity and room for future expansion.

Plan

A migration strategy built around your production schedule — phased implementation for large multi-PLC systems, or a straight swap where that's the right answer.

Migrate

PLC hardware and software upgraded alongside SCADA/HMI and communication networks where applicable, tested thoroughly before anything goes live.

Commission

On-site commissioning and handover with your operators and engineers, taking the lead role in plant re-commissioning so production restarts on schedule.
